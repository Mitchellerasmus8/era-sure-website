Add a photo to a category

  1. Go to app.pagescms.org, sign in with GitHub and open era-sure-website.
  2. Click Product categories, then the category (e.g. Cables).
  3. Scroll to Gallery photos and click + Add.
  4. Click the Photo box. Open the folder with the same name as the category, then click Upload and choose your picture.
  5. Fill in Description of the photo: one plain sentence about what's in it. Google reads this.
  6. Photo source: choose Era-Sure's own photo.
  7. Drag the photo up or down to change its position, then click Save.

Change text or remove a photo

  • Text: open the category, edit any box, click Save.
  • Remove a photo: open its row in Gallery photos, use the bin/remove icon, then Save. Keep at least one photo.
  • Card photo: the picture on the category's card on the home and products pages.
  • Items supplied / Brands: one item per line. Use + Add for a new line.

Add a delivery highlight

  1. Delivery highlights → Add an entry.
  2. Fill in the title, web address (e.g. limpopo-cable-supply), summary, location (province only), items and 1–8 photos.
  3. Leave it on Draft until the customer agrees. Then set Customer permission to Confirmed and Status to Published.

After you click Save

  • The live site updates in about 2–3 minutes. Refresh the page to see it.
  • Not showing? Open Netlify → Deploys. Green Published means it's done; red Failed means something was rejected.
  • A failed save cannot break the site. The previous version stays live. Undo your last change, or send Timm a screenshot of the red deploy.
  • Every change is saved in GitHub history, so nothing is ever truly lost.

Photo tips

  • Use JPG or PNG. iPhone photos are often HEIC, which won't upload. Set iPhone → Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible, or send the photo to yourself on WhatsApp or email first.
  • Landscape (sideways) photos look best. Use good light and a clean background.
  • Only use photos you took or own. Never use pictures from Google or supplier websites.
  • Blur or crop out faces, number plates and customer names, unless the customer has agreed.

Leave these to the developer

  • New pages, new categories or layout / design changes
  • Videos, and stock photos (these need licence credits)
  • Anything in Netlify's Domain or Build settings
  • Changing a web address that is already published
  • The phone number, email and address in the site footer
  • A save that fails twice in a row
