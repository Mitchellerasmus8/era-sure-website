# Third-party category photography

**No third-party category photographs remain as of 2026-09-21.** All six
categories use client-supplied photographs for their galleries and card images.

The seven electrical-consumables photographs replaced the final two stock
assets: Fructibus's CC0 blade-terminal photograph and Simon A. Eugster's
CC BY-SA 3.0 wire-ferrule photograph. Both retired assets and their gallery
credits were removed. Earlier attribution details remain in Git history.

Cable management changed to client photography on 2026-08-17; renewable-energy
equipment and switchgear followed on 2026-08-25. The register lists only what
the site currently ships.

## Relationship to the content entries (v0.12.0)

Any future third-party gallery photograph must also be recorded in its
`src/content/categories/*.yaml` entry as `provenance: third-party` plus a
`credit` block. That block renders visible attribution beneath the gallery.

**This file stays the register of record.** Edit it first when a photograph is added,
replaced or relicensed, then transcribe the values into the content entry — never the other
way round. The tables here carry the full sourcing context; the content entry carries only
what the page displays.

There is no outstanding stock-card attribution work. If a licensed stock
`cardImage` is introduced again, review its attribution requirements before use.
